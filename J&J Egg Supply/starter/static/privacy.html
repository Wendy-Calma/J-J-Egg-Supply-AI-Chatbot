Privacy Policy

J&J Egg Supply Chatbot · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the J&J Egg Supply chatbot ("the chatbot") handles your information when you chat with the J&J Egg Supply Facebook Page on Messenger or use our web chat.

Information we collect

  • Your messages to the chatbot and its replies.
  • Your Messenger ID (a number Facebook assigns to your conversation with our Page), so the chatbot can reply to you and remember the conversation.
  • Order details you choose to give us, such as your name, contact number, delivery address, the products you order, and your payment method.

We do not collect your password, payment card numbers, or any information from your Facebook profile beyond what Messenger provides to reply to you.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our eggs, prices, delivery, and store hours.
  • To record and fulfil orders you place through the chat.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Chat history and orders are stored on a computer operated by the J&J Egg Supply team. The chatbot's AI model runs on that same computer, so your messages are not sent to any outside AI company. Messages sent through Messenger are also handled by Meta Platforms under Meta's Privacy Policy. Only the J&J Egg Supply team can access the stored data.

How long we keep it

We keep chat history and order records only as long as needed to serve you and run the business, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your chat history and order records at any time. Send a message to the J&J Egg Supply Facebook Page saying "Please delete my data". We will delete your information within 30 days and confirm by message.
